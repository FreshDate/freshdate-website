2. Description of Service

FreshDate is a mobile application that helps users track food expiry dates, generate meal plans, and manage family dietary profiles. The App uses artificial intelligence to extract expiry dates from food labels and to suggest recipes based on available ingredients. Available on iOS and Android.

Key features:

AI-powered expiry date scanning from food packaging (via Manus LLM)

Food inventory tracking and management

Meal planning and recipe suggestions (powered by TheMealDB)

Multi-user household profiles

Push notifications for expiring items

Dietary preference and family profile management

4. Food Safety & AI Limitations

4.1 AI Extraction Limitations

The App uses Manus LLM to extract expiry dates from food packaging photos. AI extraction has known limitations:

May misread faint, stamped, embossed, or dot-matrix printed dates

May confuse batch codes, production dates, or other markings with expiry dates

May fail on non-English packaging or unusual date formats

Accuracy not guaranteed under all lighting or packaging conditions

You must always verify expiry dates by reading the packaging directly. The App is a convenience tool, not a replacement for personal verification.

4.2 Date Labelling Standards

Different countries and food types use different standards (Best Before, Use By, Sell By, etc.). The App attempts to categorise these, but you are responsible for understanding the meaning of each date type on your specific products.

4.3 Storage Conditions

The App does not account for improper storage, temperature abuse, or contamination. Even if an expiry date has not passed, food may be unsafe. Always use your senses — if food looks or smells unusual, discard it immediately.
