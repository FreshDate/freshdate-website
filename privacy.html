Privacy Policy

FreshDate — Food Expiry Tracker & Meal Planner
Last updated: 7 April 2026

FreshDate ("the App") is designed to help you track food expiry dates, plan meals for your family, and reduce food waste. This privacy policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

The App collects different categories of information depending on which features you use. The table below provides a complete overview of every data type the App may access.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

The vast majority of your data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's on-device key-value storage). This means your food items, family profiles, meal plans, and preferences remain on your phone and are not uploaded to any cloud database unless you explicitly use a feature that requires server processing.

Local Storage (On Your Device)

The following data never leaves your device under normal use:

Food Items Family Profiles Meal Plans Preferences Notification IDs

Secure Storage (Device Keychain / Keystore)

If you sign in, your authentication session token is stored in your device's secure enclave (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), which provides hardware-level encryption.

3. Data Transmitted to Our Server

Certain features require sending data to our server for AI-powered processing. The server processes these requests in real time and does not permanently store the data sent. The table below details each transmission.

FeatureData SentWhat Happens
Date ExtractionPhoto of food labelImage is uploaded to secure storage and analysed by AI to extract the expiry date. The image is used solely for this purpose.

Meal Plan GenerationFood item names, family member info, dietary preferencesAI creates a weekly meal plan. Data is processed in memory and not stored.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

FreshDate offers optional premium subscriptions managed through Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play Store (Android). All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see, collect, or store your payment card details, bank information, or billing address. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives only an anonymous user identifier and purchase receipts to verify entitlements.

7. Data We Do Not Collect

FreshDate does not collect, access, or transmit any of the following:

Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints • Browsing history or search history • Contacts, calendar events, or health data • Background location tracking • Call logs, SMS messages, or microphone audio • Financial or payment card information • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete individual food items, family members, or meal plans directly within the App. To remove all App data at once, use the "Delete Account" option in Settings, which clears all local data, cancels scheduled notifications, and requests server-side account deletion if you are signed in. Alternatively, you can uninstall the App or clear its storage through your device's settings. Server-side AI processing is stateless — data sent for recipe generation or date extraction is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the response is returned.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Authentication tokens are stored in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Photos uploaded for date extraction are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in encrypted cloud storage. All communication between the App and our server uses TLS encryption.

10. Children's Privacy

FreshDate is a family-oriented app that allows parents to create profiles for children as part of meal planning. The App does not independently collect data from children. All family member profiles, including those for children, are created and managed by the adult user of the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

11. Your Rights

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you inherently have full access to, and control over, all of your information. You may view, edit, or delete any data at any time through the App's interface. If you have signed in and wish to delete your account, you may do so through Settings > Delete Account. Under GDPR (UK/EU) and similar regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data. For any data-related requests that cannot be handled through the App, please contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features or applicable regulations. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
