Features

Built for families who
buy food every week

Six tools that work together — from the moment you unpack your shopping to the moment you sit down to eat.

AI Camera Scanner

Point your camera at any food label. FreshDate reads the expiry date — even faded dot-matrix stamps on milk cartons — and adds the item to your tracker in seconds.

Expiry Calendar

See your entire fridge and freezer on a monthly calendar. Colour-coded dots show what needs using first. Tap any date to see what expires.

AI Meal Planning

Generate a full week of recipes built around the food you already have — prioritising items that expire soonest. Every recipe includes detailed step-by-step instructions.

Smart Notifications

Alerts fire 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the day food expires. FreshDate suggests freezing items that are still safe — and never suggests it for eggs, cream, or salad.

Money Saved & Wasted

Note what your shopping cost and FreshDate keeps score. Eat something in time and it counts towards money saved; let it go off and it counts as wasted. Every item is listed, so you can see where it goes.

Smart Shopping List

Build your list in seconds. Add ingredients from any recipe with one tap. Estimated expiry dates are pre-filled so your next shop is already tracked before you get home.
