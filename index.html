What FreshDate does

Everything your household needs
to stop wasting food

Six core features, built to work together — from the moment you unpack your shopping to the moment you sit down to eat.

AI Camera Scanner

Point your camera at any food label — including faded dot-matrix stamps on milk cartons and foil packaging. FreshDate reads the expiry date automatically and adds the item to your tracker in seconds.

Expiry Calendar

See your entire fridge and freezer on a monthly calendar. Colour-coded dots show what needs using first. Tap any date to see exactly what expires and get instant cooking suggestions.

AI Meal Planning

Generate a full week of recipes built around the food you already have — prioritising items that expire soonest. Every recipe includes step-by-step instructions and per-serving nutrition.

Family Profiles

Add every member of your household with their age, dietary preferences, and restrictions. FreshDate tailors meal suggestions and calorie targets to each person — from toddlers to teenagers.

Smart Shopping List

Build your shopping list in seconds. Add ingredients directly from any recipe with one tap. Estimated expiry dates are pre-filled for every item so your next shop is already tracked before you get home.

Smart Notifications

Receive timely alerts 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the day food expires. Notifications suggest freezing items that are still safe to freeze — and never suggest it for eggs, cream, or salad.

Freezer management

Freeze smarter.
Defrost safely.

FreshDate knows exactly how long every food type can stay frozen — and tells you the safe window to eat it after defrosting.

  • ❄️

    Swipe to freeze

    Swipe left on any item and tap Freeze. FreshDate automatically calculates the frozen shelf life — chicken gets 3 months, butter 9 months, bread 3 months.

  • 🌡️

    Safe defrost windows

    When you defrost, FreshDate sets a safe eating deadline based on UK Food Standards Agency guidelines. Raw poultry: 24 hours. Red meat: 48 hours. Butter: 7 days.

  • ⚠️

    Max-freeze warnings

    A notification fires 7 days before a frozen item reaches its maximum safe storage time — so nothing gets forgotten at the back of the freezer.
